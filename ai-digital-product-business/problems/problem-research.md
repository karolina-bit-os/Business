## How to read this

**Evidence labels**

| Label | Meaning here |
|---|---|
| **PUBLIC FACT** | A figure or statement from a published study or article, **as cited in your research docs**. The original sources have **not** been re-checked in this pass. |
| **OBSERVATION** | Something actually recorded: your own survey answers, or a specific forum post or review someone read and logged. One observation = one person. |
| **INFERENCE** | An interpretation drawn from observations or facts (including interpretations already written in your research docs). Not evidence on its own. |
| **UNKNOWN** | Not available in the research. |

**Research ratings** (not product verdicts): STRONG RESEARCH CANDIDATE · WORTH MORE RESEARCH · WEAK SIGNAL · NOT ENOUGH DATA
