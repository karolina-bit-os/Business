## 1. Low energy / tiring faster than before

**PROBLEM:** Low energy during the day; tiring faster than before.

**WHO APPEARS TO EXPERIENCE IT:** 8 of 11 survey respondents (mostly women 35–44). In a public study: women in leadership positions.

**SPECIFIC SITUATION:** Daily-life impairment, median 6/10 across all 11 respondents (not only this group). One respondent described stress, waking at night and poor regeneration. Other detail: UNKNOWN.

**CURRENT SOLUTION / WORKAROUND:** Survey (all 11): sport (7), diet change (6), supplements (6), doctor visit (6), coaching or course (1), nothing (2).

**FRUSTRATION:** Doctors who wave it off or know too little; contradictory information; changes that don't last (S1 themes). One respondent: standard tips like meditation don't work when the nervous system is too overstimulated.

**DESIRED OUTCOME:** Respondent wording: „keine Müdigkeit tagsüber". Anything beyond that: UNKNOWN. The Energie-Guide's "more energy across the day" is your hypothesis, not a respondent statement.

**SOURCE / WHERE OBSERVED:** S1; S2 (Women InChange study).

**REPETITION SIGNAL:** 8 of 11, the most-named topic in your survey. Also appears in a separate public study.

**EVIDENCE:** Respondents' words: „Müdigkeit", „werde schneller müde", „viel weniger Energie als sonst". Women InChange (Healthcare Frauen e.V., n=821, March 2025): physical exhaustion 76.4%.

**EVIDENCE LABEL:** OBSERVATION (survey) · PUBLIC FACT (study, as cited in S2)

**UNKNOWNS:** Whether it is respondents' biggest problem or one of several; whether respondents match your target customer; whether your Instagram/TikTok followers share it; whether anyone would pay for a solution to it specifically.

**RESEARCH STATUS:** **STRONG RESEARCH CANDIDATE**. It is the most repeated signal in your own data, it matches an independent study, and 6 respondents can be contacted.

## 2. Symptoms dismissed or misread as "just stress"

**PROBLEM:** Symptoms are attributed to stress or age ("too young"), and women don't feel taken seriously by doctors.

**WHO APPEARS TO EXPERIENCE IT:** Some survey respondents (named as a frustration theme; count UNKNOWN). Women in German forum and blog reports (S2).

**SPECIFIC SITUATION:** At the gynaecologist: "your period is still regular, so it can't be perimenopause" (mal-ehrlich.ch, quoted in S2). Women also read their own symptoms as stress (S2).

**CURRENT SOLUTION / WORKAROUND:** Seeing a doctor (6 of 11 in S1). Books, podcasts, social media, Google (S1 information sources).

**FRUSTRATION:** „tatsächlich ernst genommen wurde ich nie" (Remifemin blog comment, quoted in S2). S1: doctors who wave it off.

**DESIRED OUTCOME:** From quotes in S2: knowing what is going on („froh, zu wissen, dass es die Wechseljahre sind, dass ich nicht todkrank bin") and being taken seriously.

**SOURCE / WHERE OBSERVED:** S1; S2 (DAK 2025, Essity 2025, mal-ehrlich.ch, Remifemin blog comments).

**REPETITION SIGNAL:** In your survey and in several separate first-person reports. S2 calls it a pattern "in almost every German experience report"; that is S2's judgement, and no count was logged.

**EVIDENCE:** DAK 2025 (n=2,500 working women): 91% expect information from their gynaecologist, 19% receive it; 35% have taken no action so far. Essity 2025: 18% of German women know what perimenopause is.

**EVIDENCE LABEL:** PUBLIC FACT (studies, as cited in S2) · OBSERVATION (survey theme, quoted reports)

**UNKNOWNS:** How many respondents named it; whether a digital product can address it (your planned doctor checklist is one untested attempt); what you may legally say while your Heilpraktikerin training is still in progress.

**RESEARCH STATUS:** **STRONG RESEARCH CANDIDATE**. It appears in your survey, in published studies and in independent first-person reports.

**FRUSTRATION:** „After being self-employed for years, I now find myself unable to work"; „It makes working and running a business feel very uncertain" (quotes in S2). One Reddit poster estimated lost income in the tens of thousands of pounds (her own unverified figure, S3). Another self-employed commenter dislikes more funnels and constant growth pressure (S3).

**DESIRED OUTCOME:** Being reliable again, able to commit to a launch, a project or a week (S2). „the old me" (S2).

**SOURCE / WHERE OBSERVED:** S2 and S3 (Reddit r/Menopause, Business Insider, Substack comments, Kontist article).

**REPETITION SIGNAL:** Several separate English-language posts. Repetition among German-speaking women: UNKNOWN.

**EVIDENCE:** Quotes above. WSI (as cited in S2): two-thirds of self-employed women work without employees.

**EVIDENCE LABEL:** OBSERVATION (individual posts) · PUBLIC FACT (WSI, as cited) · INFERENCE (S2's "she buys business continuity, not health" is an interpretation)

**UNKNOWNS:** Whether any survey respondent, newsletter subscriber or follower is self-employed; whether German-speaking solo women describe the problem this way; how big this group is in your audience.

**RESEARCH STATUS:** **WORTH MORE RESEARCH**. The public signals are real but mostly English-language. Your current strategy is built on this problem, and your own data hasn't confirmed it yet.

## 4. Brain fog making written work and communication harder

**PROBLEM:** Concentration problems and brain fog get in the way of work tasks such as emails, summaries and starting to write.

**WHO APPEARS TO EXPERIENCE IT:** Women in perimenopause with knowledge-work or leadership roles (Reddit, S3). Women in leadership (Women InChange, S2).

**SPECIFIC SITUATION:** Writing emails, meeting notes and outlines; finding words. A German forum post describes word-finding problems (Urbia, S3).

**CURRENT SOLUTION / WORKAROUND:** Some use ChatGPT for emails, bullet points and summaries. One uses the paid version and took a prompt-writing course: „Taking a prompt writing course was very much time well spent for me!" (S3). They check drafts and keep confidential data out.

**FRUSTRATION:** Brain fog affecting job performance ("Ruining my career" thread, S3).

**DESIRED OUTCOME:** UNKNOWN beyond getting the work done.

**SOURCE / WHERE OBSERVED:** S3 (Reddit: "Peri Brain and ChatGPT", "Ruining my career"; Urbia); S2 (Women InChange).

**REPETITION SIGNAL:** Two Reddit threads with several commenters, plus one German forum post. Not asked about in your survey.

**EVIDENCE:** Posts above. Women InChange (as cited in S2): concentration problems 81.9%; 58.1% report negative effects on their work or leadership performance.

**EVIDENCE LABEL:** OBSERVATION (posts) · PUBLIC FACT (study, as cited) · INFERENCE (S3's conclusion that a narrow workload-relief offer is "most plausible")

**UNKNOWNS:** Whether German-speaking or self-employed women do this; whether your audience has this problem; whether anyone would pay for it (one person's paid course is not demand).

**RESEARCH STATUS:** **WORTH MORE RESEARCH**. It is the only entry where someone is observed already paying for a workaround, and it overlaps with your AI skills. But that rests on a few English-language threads.
