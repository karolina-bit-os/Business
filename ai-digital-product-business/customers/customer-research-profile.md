**Labels:** every statement carries exactly one label.

# SEGMENT B: Self-employed women in perimenopause running a business alone

**Linked problems:** entries 2, 3, 5, 13 in `problems/problem-research.md`
**Evidence warning:** almost entirely public desk research (S2, S3). Most first-person business quotes are **English-language**. None of your own data confirms this segment yet.

## CUSTOMER / SEGMENT
- Self-employed women, without a team, experiencing perimenopause symptoms. `OBSERVATION` (individual forum posts and articles)
- Two-thirds of self-employed women in Germany work without employees (WSI, as cited in S2). `PUBLIC FACT`
- Nearly 30% of solo self-employed people earn under €1,100 net (as cited in S2). `PUBLIC FACT`
- S2's assumption that the webinar target has "a few thousand euros monthly revenue". `INFERENCE`
- How many of your followers, subscribers or survey respondents are in this segment: `UNKNOWN`

## PROBLEM
- Working well below former capacity, with no backup if they drop out (entry 3). `OBSERVATION`
- Symptoms dismissed or read as stress (entry 2). `OBSERVATION`

## SITUATION / CONTEXT
- A self-employed commenter forgets to return calls and send quotes because of brain fog. `OBSERVATION`
- A founder describes doing "enough to get by" in her business (Business Insider, quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- If self-employed people are out for several weeks, it becomes existentially threatening (Kontist, as cited in S2). `PUBLIC FACT`
- Self-employed women don't even have an employer to talk to (S2: 48% of women find it uncomfortable to raise it with their employer). `INFERENCE`

## DESIRED OUTCOME
- Being reliable again: able to commit to a launch, a project or a week (S2's synthesis). `INFERENCE`
- „the old me" (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- Knowing what is going on, being taken seriously, trusting themselves again (S2's synthesis of quotes). `INFERENCE`

## PAIN POINTS
- „After being self-employed for years, I now find myself unable to work" (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- „It makes working and running a business feel very uncertain" (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- Dismissal by doctors: „tatsächlich ernst genommen wurde ich nie" (German blog comment, quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- 91% expect information from their gynaecologist, 19% receive it (DAK 2025, as cited). `PUBLIC FACT`
- One self-employed commenter dislikes more funnels, big offers and constant growth pressure (S3). `OBSERVATION`

## QUESTIONS
- „Was ist bloß los mit mir?" (German blog comment, quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- Questions about coaches' qualifications appear in the Reddit thread "Menopause coach should be a thing" (S3). `OBSERVATION`
- Questions specific to running a business through perimenopause: `UNKNOWN`

## OBJECTIONS / HESITATIONS

- Cost concerns about individual support (Reddit "Menopause coach" thread, S3). `OBSERVATION`
- Information-only courses compete with free content: one person who watched a menopause MasterClass criticised the lack of concrete steps (S3). `OBSERVATION`
- Big voices in this space are almost all doctors, so a non-doctor faces a credibility wall (S2). `INFERENCE`
- Two reviews criticise free webinars as self-promotion without enough guidance (FemCode, S3). `OBSERVATION`

## CURRENT ALTERNATIVES
- Supplements: 37% of consumers take vitamins/minerals for menopause symptoms (Innova, as cited). `PUBLIC FACT`
- Supplements often disappoint: „Magnesium und Vitamin D – keine Verbesserung" (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- Books (*Woman on Fire*, Mary Claire Haver), podcasts, Substack (S2). `OBSERVATION`
- DACH menopause courses priced €69.95–499 (S2/S4, unchecked). `PUBLIC FACT`
- Hiring coaches: one woman reports paying "thousands of dollars" to two fitness coaches without the progress she hoped for (S3). `OBSERVATION`
- **Doing nothing / pushing through:** 35% have taken no action so far (DAK 2025, all working women, not only self-employed). `PUBLIC FACT`
- „da muss man halt durch" (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`

## COST OF DOING NOTHING
- One Reddit poster estimated tens of thousands of pounds in lost income during a severe phase (her own unverified figure). `OBSERVATION`
- „unable to work" after years of self-employment (quoted in S2). `OBSERVATION`
- Several weeks out can threaten a self-employed person's existence (Kontist, as cited). `PUBLIC FACT`
- Typical or average cost for this segment: `UNKNOWN`
