# AI Digital Product Business

This is my **persistent AI Digital Product Business workspace**.

It is the single, ongoing home for building, launching, and running an AI-assisted
digital product business. Pages 1–18 will continuously build on this workspace:
each page adds to, refines, and references the work already stored here rather
than starting over.

## Structure

| Folder | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `market/` | Market landscape, niches, trends, and competitors |
| `problems/` | Customer problems and pain points worth solving |
| `customers/` | Target customers, personas, and audience insights |
| `research/` | Raw and synthesized research notes and sources |
| `ideas/` | Product ideas and opportunity candidates |
| `validation/` | Tests, signals, and evidence that ideas are worth building |
| `product/` | Product definition, outline, and build files |
| `offer/` | Offer design: promise, bonuses, guarantees |
| `pricing/` | Pricing strategy, tiers, and experiments |
| `sales-page/` | Sales page copy and structure |
| `content/` | Marketing content and content plans |
| `launch/` | Launch plans, checklists, and timelines |
| `delivery/` | How the product is delivered and accessed |
| `feedback/` | Customer feedback, reviews, and testimonials |
| `analytics/` | Metrics, reports, and performance tracking |
| `operations/` | Day-to-day business processes and SOPs |
| `automation/` | Automations, workflows, and tool integrations |
| `resources/` | Templates, references, and reusable assets |

## Working rules

- Every page builds on what already exists here.
- Progress and decisions are recorded in [`PROJECT-LOG.md`](PROJECT-LOG.md).
- No new permanent top-level folders unless they become technically necessary.
